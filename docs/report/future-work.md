# 5. Future Work

Semester 2 will turn the virtual prototype into hardware and test it against the targets. Table 11 gives the milestones and Table 12 the test plan.

<div markdown style="text-align: center;">
**Table 11:** Semester 2 milestones.
</div>

| Weeks | Milestone | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Confirm ArianeTech tray layout, row pitch and stem height; freeze specifications | Imran |
| 2–4 | Waterjet cutting rig; test T1 | Ignatius |
| 3–6 | Print half-cups and S-rails; build rack-and-pinion holder; test T2 | Clement |
| 5–8 | Conveyor indexing and motion control; test T4 | Imran |
| 7–9 | Tip-over and landing damage; test T3 | Imran |
| 9–11 | Integrated station; trial T5 with ArianeTech lettuce | Clement |
| 12–13 | Final report and demonstration | All |

<div markdown style="text-align: center;">
**Table 12:** Semester 2 test plan. Pass criteria are proposed and will be agreed with the supervisors and ArianeTech.
</div>

| Test | Purpose | Method | Measure and proposed pass criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 Jet cut | Find a pressure and nozzle that sever the stem | Vary pressure, nozzle diameter and stand-off; 10 stems per setting | Complete separation in at least 9 of 10 cuts |
| T2 Cup force | Find a safe closing force | Force-sensing resistor in the cup; bruise inspection after 24 h | Highest force with no visible bruising |
| T3 Landing | Check damage when heads tip onto the tray | Tip heads at the design angle; inspect leaves | No torn or bruised leaves |
| T4 Indexing | Verify cycle time and row positioning | 50 indexing cycles | Cycle at or below 8 s; stems inside the 44 mm entry gap |
| T5 Integrated trial | Measure rate and damage against manual work | At least 30 ArianeTech heads | At least 10× the manual rate; damage no worse than manual |
