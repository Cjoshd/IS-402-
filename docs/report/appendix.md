
# 8. Appendices

## 8.1 Appendix A: Jet Calculations for Four Pressure Washers

The relations presented in Section 3.7.2 were applied to
each pressure washer using its maximum rated pressure and
flow rate. The calculations assume that the entire flow is
directed through a single nozzle, with negligible losses.

The equivalent stress was calculated by distributing the
jet impact force over the cross-sectional area of a
10 mm diameter lettuce stem (78.5 mm²), following the
impact model adopted by the IS-404 Team (2026).

The reference design stress is 0.30 MPa, based on a
failure stress of 0.20 MPa and a safety factor of 1.5.
